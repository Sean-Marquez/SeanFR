ILOVEYOU VIRUS

The ILOVEYOU virus, also known as the Love Bug or Love Letter, was a computer worm that spread through email in May 2000. It was one of the most damaging computer viruses ever created, causing billions of dollars in damages worldwide.

The virus was disguised as a love letter and tricked users into opening an attachment, which then executed malicious code that overwrote files and sent copies of itself to everyone in the victim's email address book.
